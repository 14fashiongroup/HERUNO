Your Order Journey

From first enquiry to delivery.

Lead times typically begin once your branding has been approved and the agreed deposit has been received.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your project

    We'll discuss your organisation, branding requirements and order quantity.

  2. 2

    We'll prepare your branding proof

    You'll receive a digital artwork proof showing how your logo will appear on HERUNO HALO. If required, we can also discuss providing a physical HERUNO sample during the process.

  3. 3

    Approve your branding

    Once you're happy with the artwork, we'll ask for your final approval before production begins.

  4. 4

    Production

    Your order moves into production using your approved branding.

  5. 5

    Delivery

    Your finished order is delivered directly to your organisation.
