Made to be used. Designed to be remembered.

Built for the environments your organisation already operates in.

HERUNO HALO works wherever people carry bags and need somewhere practical to put them while they socialise, relax or go about their day.

Here's how it fits into some everyday settings.

Restaurants and Bars

Keeps bags off the floor, chairs, laps and tables.

Diners often have nowhere practical to put a bag. HERUNO HALO fixes to the tabletop, giving them somewhere to hang it, within easy reach and out of the way. The weight of the bag keeps the HALO in place, stopping it from falling off.

  • Frees up seating instead of using it for bags
  • Keeps bags visible and within the diner's reach
  • Branding stays at eye level throughout the meal
  • Hygienic option for diners

Hotels

Enhances guest experience with a small everyday luxury.

Guests move through several spaces during a stay, often carrying bags with nowhere suitable to rest them. HERUNO HALO gives hotels a practical fixture for bars, lobbies and function rooms, keeping the brand visible at every stage of their visit (and beyond).

  • Useful across multiple spaces in one property
  • Suits both guest areas and event or conference rooms
  • Reinforces brand presence beyond the guest's room

Offices

Keeps bags off the floor at desks and in meeting rooms.

Bags left on the floor create clutter and a trip hazard around desks and meeting tables. HERUNO HALO gives employees and visitors a fixed place to hang a bag, keeping workspaces and walkways clear.

  • Reduces clutter around desks and shared tables
  • Works well in meeting and reception areas
  • A small, practical addition to any workplace
  • Gives the workplace a forward-thinking edge

Events

Keeps your brand visible long after an event takes place.

Event managers add HERUNO HALO to their existing promotional merchandise collection, alongside things like notebooks, pens and USBs.

  • Finds a place at registration desks, exhibition stands and event tote bags
  • Keeps aisles and seating areas clear of bags
  • Branding stays visible for the length of the event and beyond
  • Sparks genuine curiosity towards your brand
