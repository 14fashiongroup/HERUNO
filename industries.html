Industries

Built for the environments your organisation already operates in.

HERUNO HALO works wherever people carry bags and need somewhere practical to put them down. Here's how it fits into four everyday settings.

Contact Sales View Products

Restaurants

Keeps bags off the floor and off chairs at the table.

Diners often have nowhere practical to put a bag. HERUNO HALO fixes to the underside of the table, giving them somewhere to hang it — off the floor, off the seat beside them, and within easy reach.

  • Frees up seating instead of using it for bags
  • Keeps bags visible and within the diner's reach
  • Branding stays at eye level throughout the meal

Hotels

Present in lobbies, bars and event spaces throughout a guest's stay.

Guests move through several spaces during a stay, often carrying bags with nowhere suitable to rest them. HERUNO HALO gives hotels a practical fixture for bars, lobbies and function rooms, keeping the brand visible at every stage of the visit.

  • Useful across multiple spaces in one property
  • Suits both guest areas and event or conference rooms
  • Reinforces brand presence beyond the guest's room

Offices

Keeps bags off the floor at desks and in meeting rooms.

Bags left on the floor create clutter and a trip hazard around desks and meeting tables. HERUNO HALO gives employees and visitors a fixed place to hang a bag, keeping workspaces and walkways clear.

  • Reduces clutter around desks and shared tables
  • Works well in meeting and reception areas
  • A small, practical addition to any workplace

Events

Keeps your brand visible at registration desks and seating areas.

Events bring bags, badges and belongings into spaces that weren't designed to hold them. HERUNO HALO gives organisers a practical fixture for registration desks, exhibition stands and seating areas — keeping the brand visible for the full duration of the event.

  • Suits registration desks and exhibition stands
  • Keeps aisles and seating areas clear of bags
  • Branding stays visible for the length of the event
